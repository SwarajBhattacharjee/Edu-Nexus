SECTION 1: DOCUMENT CONTROL, SAFETY BRIEFINGS, AND LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 PURPOSE:
    This manual specifies direct, step-by-step physical and command-line protocols
    for commissioning, recalibrating, diagnosing, and safely recovering AMR v4.2
    units operating within active customer warehouse and assembly environments.

1.2 MANDATORY HIGH-VOLTAGE & KINETIC SAFETY RULES:
    * DANGER: The AMR chassis houses a 48V / 60Ah LiFePO4 battery pack capable of
      discharging up to 180A peak current. Short circuits present an instantaneous
      arc-flash and thermal runaway risk.
    * ALWAYS disengage the main DC rotary isolator switch (located on the rear
      lower service hatch) and apply a physical lockout padlock prior to removing
      chassis top plates or servicing motor drives.
    * NEVER attempt to bypass the mechanical Emergency Stop (E-Stop) buttons or
      wire jumpers across the safety relay terminals.
    * Wear ANSI Z87.1 certified safety glasses, steel-toed ESD footwear, and
      cut-resistant Level 3 gloves during all mechanical and electrical operations.

SECTION 2: SITE COMMISSIONING & PRE-FLIGHT HARDWARE INSPECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Execute this checklist sequentially prior to clearing any new or serviced AMR
for autonomous mission engagement:

[ ] 2.1 PHYSICAL CHASSIS & FASTENER AUDIT:
    - Inspect drive-wheel polyurethane treads. Ensure minimum tread depth > 3.5mm.
    - Verify drive wheel hub bolts (M8 grade 8.8) are torqued to exactly 24.5 Nm.
    - Check front and rear passive swivel casters for debris, thread entanglement,
      and ensure free 360-degree rotation.
    - Verify safety bumper rubber skirt is uncompromised and returns smoothly
      when physically depressed on left, center, and right quadrants.

[ ] 2.2 OPTICAL & SENSOR APERTURE SANITIZATION:
    - Clean the primary SICK safety LiDAR optical hood using a lint-free microfiber
      cloth dampened with 99% pure electronic-grade Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).
    - CAUTION: Do NOT use acetone or industrial degreasers on LiDAR domes; solvent
      will craze the polycarbonate optical surface and cause false scattering.
    - Inspect the front Intel RealSense RGB-D stereo camera lens protective glass.
      Ensure zero oil smudges, dust deposition, or surface abrasions.

[ ] 2.3 POWER-ON & ELECTRICAL RAIL VALIDATION:
    - Rotate rear DC isolation switch clockwise to "ON".
    - Observe front LED status bar: Must transition from SOLID ORANGE (booting)
      to PULSING BLUE (self-test) within 28 seconds.
    - Measure test-point TP1 (24V logic rail) on the motherboard: 24.1V +/- 0.4V.
    - Measure test-point TP2 (12V sensor rail): 12.05V +/- 0.2V.

SECTION 3: EDGE TERMINAL DIAGNOSTICS & TELEMETRY TRIAGE CHEATSHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Field technicians must connect via Ethernet service port (IP: 192.168.10.200/24)
or service Wi-Fi and execute diagnostics through the local edge terminal:

Command Reference:
------------------
1. Check Global System Health & Hardware Daemons:
   $ amr-health --summary
   $ systemctl status amr-core.service
   $ journalctl -u amr-core.service -n 50 --no-pager

2. Verify Sensor Ingestion & Sample Frequency:
   $ ros2 topic hz /sensors/lidar_front/scan
     Expected: average rate: 33.32 Hz | min: 0.028s | max: 0.033s | std dev: 0.0012s
   $ ros2 topic hz /sensors/imu/data_raw
     Expected: average rate: 1000.1 Hz +/- 2 Hz
   $ ros2 topic hz /localization/odom_filtered
     Expected: average rate: 100.0 Hz

3. Verify Edge AI GPU Inference Pipeline:
   $ nvtop
     Verify: TensorRT worker process PID is active.
     Memory usage: ~4.2 GB / 32 GB allocated.
     GPU Temp: < 72°C (Warning threshold: 82°C; Thermal cut: 90°C).

4. Verify Low-Level CAN Bus Communication Integrity:
   $ ip -details link show can0
     Check: State must be "UP". Bitrate: 1000000.
   $ candump can0 -n 10
     Verify: Rapid burst of 0x100, 0x180, 0x200, 0x280 messages.
   $ ip -s -d link show can0 | grep -E "RX|TX|errors"
     Check: bus-off counter must equal 0, error-warning counter must equal 0.

SECTION 4: STEP-BY-STEP FIELD SENSOR RECALIBRATION ROUTINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 IMU ZERO-POINT GYROSCOPE BIAS CALIBRATION (Run if robot drifts angularly):
    Step 1: Park AMR on a verified level granite surface plate or flat concrete.
    Step 2: Ensure zero floor vibration (disable nearby conveyor belts).
    Step 3: Put robot into maintenance mode:
            $ amr-cli mode --set MAINTENANCE
    Step 4: Execute IMU bias capture routine (takes 60 seconds):
            $ ros2 run amr_calibration calibrate_imu_bias --samples 60000 --save
    Step 5: Inspect output:
            Gyroscope Bias X: < 0.0012 rad/s
            Gyroscope Bias Y: < 0.0009 rad/s
            Gyroscope Bias Z: < 0.0008 rad/s
    Step 6: Restart state estimator:
            $ systemctl restart amr-localization.service

4.2 WHEEL EFFECTIVE DIAMETER & TRACK WIDTH CALIBRATION:
    Step 1: Lay a calibrated 10.000-meter laser distance tape on the test track.
    Step 2: Command the robot to drive exactly 10.000 meters along a straight tape:
            $ ros2 run amr_calibration drive_linear_distance --meters 10.00
    Step 3: Measure actual physical distance stopped using laser rangefinder.
    Step 4: Calculate scale factor: K_linear = D_actual / 10.000.
    Step 5: If K_linear != 1.0000 (+/- 0.002), update `/etc/amr/kinematics.yaml`:
            wheel_radius_left: 0.10025 * K_linear
            wheel_radius_right: 0.10025 * K_linear

SECTION 6: EMERGENCY MANUAL OVERRIDE & DEAD-ROBOT EXTRACTION PLAYBOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If an AMR suffers complete electrical shutdown or mechanical lockout in a high-
traffic main forklift aisle, execute this rapid clearance procedure:

6.1 MECHANICAL BRAKE MANUAL RELEASE:
    1. Turn OFF main DC rotary switch.
    2. Insert the specialized M5 T-handle brake release tool into the two red
       apertures on the lower chassis rear panel.
    3. Rotate both T-handles 90 degrees clockwise until an audible mechanical
       CLICK confirms the electromagnetic brake springs are compressed.
    4. The robot can now be pushed by hand.

6.2 TOWING RESTRICTIONS & DANGER WARNING:
    * CAUTION: Tow velocity MUST NOT EXCEED 0.3 meters/second (walking pace).
    * EXPLANATION: The permanent magnet synchronous motors act as electrical
      generators when back-driven. Exceeding 0.5 m/s generates back-EMF voltages
      exceeding 60V, which will instantly blow the motor controller inverter FETs.
    * Once safely parked in the maintenance bay, IMMEDIATELY rotate the T-handles
      90 degrees counter-clockwise to re-engage the mechanical fail-safe brakes.

SECTION 7: PREVENTATIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE & CADENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Daily Shift Handover (10 Minutes per unit):
- Wipe all LiDAR and camera optical lenses with optical cloth and 99% IPA.
- Visual inspection of polyurethane drive tire perimeter for embedded debris.
- Battery SOC check: Ensure pack sits above 30% before shift launch.

Weekly Maintenance (45 Minutes per unit):
- Clean chassis cooling air intake foam filters with industrial vacuum.
- Execute automated software self-test: `$ amr-test --subsystems all`.
- Inspect battery cell voltage delta via `$ amr-bms --cells`. Maximum allowable
  variance between cell 1 and cell 16 is 0.035V. If higher, initiate a 6-hour
  slow balancing charge cycle.

Monthly Maintenance (2 Hours per unit):
- Retorque drive wheel bolts, caster swivel mounts, and chassis lift screws.
- Measure wheel ground contact resistance (ESD grounding path < 1.0 MOhm).
- Archive local blackbox telemetry files and clear `/var/log/amr/` partition.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8: INCIDENT ESCALATION, LOG CAPTURE, AND TIER-3 DISPATCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If a robot exhibits an unresolved recurring crash (more than 2 stops per shift):

8.1 EXTRACT BLACKBOX MCAP TELEMETRY DUMP:
    Insert an encrypted FAT32/exFAT USB rescue drive into the service port:
    $ amr-export-blackbox --last-hours 4 --output /media/usb0/
    Verify creation of `.mcap` bag file and `syslog.tar.gz`.

8.2 SEVERITY LEVEL CLASSIFICATION & SLA RESPONSE:
    - Severity 1 (Safety Incident / Injury Risk / Battery Smoke):
      Action: Push E-Stop, evacuate 5m radius, notify Site Safety Director.
      SLA Response: Immediate (15 minutes). Hotline: +1 (800) 555-AMR-HELP opt 1.
    - Severity 2 (Complete Fleet Halted / Navigation Blocked):
      Action: Export blackbox, ping on-call AMR Site Reliability Engineer.
      SLA Response: 1 Hour.
    - Severity 3 (Single AMR Out of Service, fleet operational):
      Action: Park in maintenance bay, open Tier-2 Jira ticket with blackbox attachment.
      SLA Response: 4 Hours.
